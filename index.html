About GroupChatOn

GroupChatOn started as a simple directory of WhatsApp groups and has grown into a family of six distinct services used by students, small-business owners, readers, and working professionals across India. Everything is built as static pages on GitHub — no servers, no databases, no recurring infrastructure costs — which is what makes it possible to keep every service genuinely free, indefinitely.

Each service is designed to work well on a low-end Android phone on a slow data connection. Tools load offline, novels sync in the background, guides are written in plain language, and office templates open in the copy of Excel or Word already on your computer. We support 24 Indian languages across the GST tools and billing software, so you can work in the language you think in.

There are no paid plans, no premium tiers, and no features locked behind a subscription. Sponsor cards on a few guide pages are the only way the sites earn anything, and they never appear inside a tool, on a print page, or while you are actively working. The goal is simply to build things that are genuinely useful — and then leave them free forever.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using GroupChatOn services.

Are all GroupChatOn services really free?
Yes — every tool, guide, template, and service is completely free to use with no hidden costs. There are no paid plans or premium features. The only ads are sponsor cards on a small number of guide pages; they never appear inside a tool, on a printed document, or while you are actively working. This will not change.
Do I need to create an account?
No account is ever required for any GroupChatOn service. You open the site and start using it immediately. There is no sign-up, no email address needed, and no password to remember. Your settings and saved data are stored locally in your own browser.
Do the services work without an internet connection?
Yes. Infinity (online tools), the novels reading app, and the billing tools all work offline after you visit them once. The browser caches the pages and scripts so you can open them without data. Some features — like loading a new chapter or fetching a live exchange rate — naturally need a connection, but the core tool or document always works offline.
Which Indian languages are supported?
The billing and document tools support 24 languages: Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Urdu, Assamese, Maithili, Bhojpuri, Nepali, Konkani, Dogri, Sindhi, Kashmiri, Bodo, Santali, Manipuri, and Sanskrit, plus English. Help articles are available in Hindi, Punjabi, and Bhojpuri.
Is my data stored on GroupChatOn servers?
No. GroupChatOn is built entirely as static pages — there are no servers that store your personal data. Any information you type into a tool or save in the billing app stays inside your own browser's storage on your device. For file transfers via Send, files travel peer-to-peer directly between the two browsers and are never uploaded to any server.
What is the Infinity PWA?
Infinity is a Progressive Web App — a website you can install on your phone or computer like a native app. Once installed, it appears on your home screen or taskbar, loads instantly, and works offline. To install it, open infinity.groupchaton.com in Chrome or Edge and tap the "Install app" prompt at the bottom of the screen.
How does Send transfer files securely?
Send uses WebRTC — the same technology that powers video calls in your browser. When you share the link, a direct encrypted peer-to-peer channel is set up between the two devices. Files flow through this channel in encrypted chunks without passing through any server, and the channel closes as soon as the transfer finishes.
Can I use GroupChatOn tools for my business?
Yes. The Infinity tools, Toolkit templates, and Nivesh Sahayak document tools (at niveshsahayak.in) are all suitable for business use. The billing and GST tools produce GST-compliant tax invoices, support 24 languages, and generate GSTR-1 summaries and Tally exports. All are free for commercial use.
