About GroupChatOn

GroupChatOn started as a site for Korean web novel fans and has grown into a free multi-purpose platform serving readers, professionals, and everyday internet users across India and beyond. Everything here is free to use — no account, no subscription, and no hidden costs.

The name "GroupChatOn" began with a WhatsApp group directory (now at Share) and a novel blog. Today the platform includes a full reading app at novel.groupchaton.com, a 600+ tool collection at Infinity, how-to guides at Help, and professional office templates at Toolkit.

All tools and content are free forever. We keep the lights on through carefully placed sponsor cards and optional support — never paywalls or mandatory sign-ups.
