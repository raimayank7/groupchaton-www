Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

GroupChatOn ("we", "our") is a family of free web services built and maintained by an individual creator. This policy explains how we handle information when you use www.groupchaton.com and its subdomains (Infinity, Share, Help, Toolkit, Send, App).

What we collect

We do not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address, or payment details.

  • Usage analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless, no personal identifiers) to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are popular. No personal data leaves your browser.
  • Local storage. Tools, settings, theme preference, and saved documents are stored only in your own browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). We cannot read this data — it never leaves your device.
  • Shared data home. If you use document tools (Billing, Quotation, etc.) on Nivesh Sahayak sites, your data is stored in a shared browser-based vault tied to that domain. Same rule applies: it stays in your browser.
  • Contact messages. If you contact us via the contact form, your message, name, and optional reply address are received and stored by us for up to 12 months, then deleted. We use it only to respond to you.
  • Group submissions (Share). If you submit a WhatsApp group or channel link via Share, the link, description, and category you provide are stored publicly in the directory. You can request removal at any time.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies. Your theme choice and tool settings are saved in your own browser's localStorage, not sent to us.

Google AdSense may serve ads on some pages. AdSense uses cookies to personalise ads — see Google's ad policy. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — font files served from fonts.googleapis.com; Google may log the request IP.
  • Cloudflare — CDN and analytics; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense — advertising on select pages; see Google's privacy policy.
  • Disqus — comments on help.groupchaton.com articles; Disqus has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You may request deletion of any personally identifiable information we hold (contact messages or group submissions) by writing to us at the contact page. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the services after an update constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us.
